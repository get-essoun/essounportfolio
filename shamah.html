Project Overview

Shamah Company Limited is a technology-driven environmental services company focused on modernizing waste collection and management in Ghana. The company connects households, businesses, institutions, and residential communities with verified private waste collectors through a digital platform.

For this project, I developed the brand identity and visual direction for Shamah, creating a professional and modern identity that communicates cleanliness, reliability, technology, and environmental responsibility.

The branding was designed to establish Shamah as more than a waste collection service — but as a technology-enabled environmental solutions company with a clear and trustworthy presence across its digital products and physical touchpoints.

Design Direction

The visual identity combines clean, modern aesthetics with environmental cues, creating a system that feels approachable while maintaining a professional corporate character. The identity was developed to work consistently across the Shamah mobile application, website, social media, marketing materials, uniforms, vehicles, and other brand touchpoints.

Objective

The primary objective was to create a distinctive and scalable brand identity that would help Shamah build trust with customers and waste collectors while positioning the company as a forward-thinking environmental technology brand.
