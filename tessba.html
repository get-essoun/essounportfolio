Project Overview

Tessba Multimedia is a multimedia brand specializing in sports photography, videography, and motion graphics. The brand focuses on capturing dynamic moments, documenting experiences, and transforming them into engaging visual stories for individuals, teams, organizations, and businesses.

For this project, I developed the brand identity and visual direction for Tessba Multimedia, creating a bold and professional identity that communicates creativity, energy, movement, and visual excellence.

Design Direction

The visual identity combines a bold, energetic aesthetic with a clean and modern visual language. Strong typography, high-contrast black and white, dynamic compositions, and the vibrant #DA0001 accent colour create a confident identity that reflects the energy of sports and the fast-paced nature of multimedia production.

Objective

The primary objective was to create a distinctive and scalable brand identity that would establish Tessba Multimedia as a professional and memorable multimedia brand. The identity aims to communicate creativity, professionalism, uniqueness, and energy while creating a strong visual presence that can support the brand's growth within the multimedia industry.

Role: Brand Designer Project Type: Brand Identity Industry: Photography & Videography Location: Ghana
